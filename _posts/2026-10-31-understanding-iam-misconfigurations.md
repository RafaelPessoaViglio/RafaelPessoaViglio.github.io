## Overview

Everyone knows that the cloud is currently the main way to run infrastructure for many companies around the world, making service management easier through a wide variety of tools and features. However, to maintain a resilient cloud architecture, it is necessary to monitor every single configuration step—focusing especially on over-privileged roles granted to IAM (Identity and Access Management) users. 

This is undoubtedly one of the biggest security challenges companies face in the current digital landscape. When attackers exploit misconfigured IAM policies, they can easily cause severe damage to an enterprise's services, core infrastructure, and global reputation. 

## Prevention and Technical Security Standards

To prevent these vectors from being exploited, organizations must prioritize deep technical knowledge in cloud security and implement proactive prevention measures. Adopting principles such as Least Privilege, enforcing multi-factor authentication, and conducting continuous permission audits are essential steps to securing cloud environments against unauthorized access.
